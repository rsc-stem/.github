# Welcome to STEM Initiatives @ Rio Salado College👋

The STEM Initiatives department has many programs for students to learn things from programming, computer science, optics and lasers, and semiconductor manufacturing. We have work with industry partners closely to ensure our programs meet their needs for graduates of our program. 

Find more about our programs on Rio Salado College's website:
* [Semiconductor Manufacturing](https://www.riosalado.edu/rio-specialized-programs/semiconductor) for an overview of the program and the grants
* [Semiconductor Manufacturing AAS degree](https://www.riosalado.edu/degrees-certificates/science-technology-engineering-and-mathematics/semiconductor-manufacturing-3168-aas)
* [Precision Optics Level I](https://www.riosalado.edu/degrees-certificates/applied-technology/precision-optics-level-i-5284n-ccl)
* [Programming and Systems Analysis AAS](https://www.riosalado.edu/degrees-certificates/computer-and-information-technology/programming-and-systems-analysis-3844-aas)
